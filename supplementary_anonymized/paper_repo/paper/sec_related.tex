\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\textbf{The loop the proposer is characterized for.} Language Model Crossover
\citep{meyerson2024languagemodelcrossovervariation}, ELM
\citep{lehman2022evolutionlargemodels}, EvoPrompt \citep{guo2024evoprompt}, EoH
\citep{liu2024eoh} and LLaMEA \citep{vanstein2025llamealargelanguagemodel} made the LLM call
an evolutionary operator over prompts and programs. FunSearch
\citep{romeraparedes2024funsearch} turned the pattern into a discovery claim. AlphaEvolve
\citep{novikov2025alphaevolve} and its successors inherit that claim, OpenEvolve
\citep{sharma2025openevolve} among them
\citep{lange2025shinkaevolveopenendedsampleefficientprogram,su2026helixevolutionaryreinforcementlearning,khrulkov2025gigaevoopensourceoptimization,cemri2026adaevolveadaptivellmdriven,wang2025thetaevolvetesttimelearningopen}.
Every one conditions its proposer on a parent and iterates it, two of the conditions this
paper varies. Simple baselines \citep{gideoni2026simplebaselinescompetitivecode} and
classical solvers \citep{berthold2026outoftheboxglobaloptimizationpacking} recover much of
the reported advantage.

\textbf{Template convergence under iteration.} ``Mutation Without Variation''
\citep{gurkan2026mutationvariationconvergencedynamics} finds iterated LLM program mutation
collapsing onto previously seen structural templates. In 87\% of chains, over 93\% of
mutations revisit a seen form. It runs mutation loops in program space with no closed form
and no preregistration, and is the closest published neighbour to arm L. Its result is about
structural revisiting. Arm L's second prediction is about a value the revisited structures
do not cross.

\textbf{Anchoring, and what kind of object anchors here.} Anchoring is studied as a general
bias over initial information, and as numeric primes dragging point estimates
\citep{huang2026understandinganchoringeffectllm,lou2024anchoringbiaslargelanguage,malberg2025comprehensiveevaluationcognitivebiases}.
The modality here differs: a construction template has a computable value, where a scalar
anchor has only a measurable pull. One level up, LLM outputs concentrate where human ones do
not \citep{chen2026measuringgaphumanllm,jiang2025artificialhivemindopenendedhomogeneity}.
Set-level and diversity-aware selection
\citep{li2026dictionariesdarwinsetlevelselection,wang2026diversescientifichypothesissearch,yang2026accuracyevaluatingstrategydiversity}
and the bin-packing critiques
\citep{herrmann2026indepthstudyllmcontributions,sim2025hypebenchmarkingllmevolvedheuristics}
converge on the same question about the proposer's contribution.

\textbf{Two results that cut against this paper's framing.} Evolutionary search matters
empirically \citep{zhang2024understandingimportanceevolutionarysearch}, so one unconditioned
call is a poor stand-in for a loop. Strong LLM optimizers act as local refiners
\citep{zhang2026makesllmgoodoptimizer}, as a parent-conditioned call does.

\textbf{Instrument effects and reasoning budget.} Larger and more instructable models become
less reliable \citep{zhou2024reliability}. A reasoning model can report a higher
hallucination rate alongside higher accuracy \citep{openai2025o3o4systemcard}, and reasoning
collapses past a complexity threshold
\citep{shojaee2025illusionthinkingunderstandingstrengths}. Geometric construction is a regime
where nominal capability and executed correctness diverge
\citep{kim2026geobuildbenchbenchmarkinteractiveexecutable,balunovic2025mathconstructchallengingllmreasoning}.

\textbf{Preregistration lineage.} Recipes, outcome-blinded predictions and agent protocols
are already preregistered
\citep{thomas2026mitigatingllmbasedphackingpreregistering,williams2026predictingllmsafetyrelease,vaccaro2026preregistrationexperimentsaiagents,zhang2026testingfrontierlargelanguage}.
HindsightBench \citep{jia2026hindsightbenchblackboxbehavioralaudit} releases frozen
preregistrations of directional aggregate hypotheses. This paper's arms lock a numeric
threshold and a falsifier before each setting was changed.
